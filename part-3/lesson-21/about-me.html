About Me

My name is Salvatore D'Angelo and I have been a professional software engineer since 1999.

I started programming in 1986 as a hobby and, before moving to professional work, I worked with several programming languages such as Turbo Pascal, C, C++, Java, SQL and Visual Basic.

I have always believed that the best way to learn a programming language or a technology is to build a small project with it. For this reason, during that period I worked on various projects including compilers, operating systems, shop management systems, and so on. Some of them are available on my GitHub page.

During that time I was attending a Computer Science school in a small nearby town. In 1992 I enrolled in the Computer Science degree at the University of Salerno and graduated with honours in 1998.

After a brief period of military service, I started working in 1999. For a couple of months I worked for a small company in Naples, then I moved to a well-known American software company in Rome, where I still work today.

At this company I have worked on many projects, collaborating on the design and implementation of numerous software products. I currently work in the Cloud area, designing and implementing resilient solutions for our platform. You can read my full career history on my LinkedIn page.
